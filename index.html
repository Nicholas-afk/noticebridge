UNDER THE BRIDGE

Small model. Visible evidence.

A trained text classifier groups each sentence into an instruction, event detail, contact or background. Rules extract dates and amounts exactly as written.

  1. Read locally. Text is processed in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent to a server.
  2. Keep the evidence. Every card points to the exact sentence in your notice. There is no AI-generated paraphrase.
  3. Show uncertainty. Missing years, relative dates and uncertain categories are highlighted for you to check.

Trained on authored synthetic examples, not real notices. Model scores are uncalibrated and do not measure factual accuracy. English only. This prototype may miss or misclassify instructions. It does not interpret legal, medical or emergency guidance.

Checks follow ideas from W3C cognitive accessibility guidance. Accessibility benefits have not been tested with users.
